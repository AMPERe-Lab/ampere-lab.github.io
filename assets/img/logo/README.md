# AMPERE Lab logo

Mark: a 12-slot stator lamination with a six-step phase voltage (steps) and its sinusoidal current (gold) in the bore.

## Folders
- `horizontal/` – icon + wordmark + tagline (also `-no-tagline`)
- `stacked/` – icon above wordmark
- `icon/` – mark only, plus app icons on navy / white tiles
- `favicon/` – simplified mark for small sizes (16–48 px)
  - `favicon.svg` switches to white automatically in dark mode:
    `<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">`
- `editable-text/` – same lockups with live text (needs the Archivo font installed)

Files outside `editable-text/` have text converted to outlines, so they look right everywhere.

## Colorways
| Name | Use on |
|---|---|
| color | white / light backgrounds |
| reversed | navy or dark backgrounds (`-on-navy` includes the navy background) |
| navy / black | one-color print, engraving, stamps |
| white | one-color on photos or dark backgrounds |

## Colors
- Navy `#0C2340`
- Gold `#C99700`
- White `#FFFFFF`

## Type
Archivo (Google Fonts, free): wordmark ExtraBold 800 (matches the website header), tagline Medium 500, letter-spaced.

## Minimum sizes
- Full mark: 48 px / 12 mm wide icon. Below that, use the favicon mark.
- Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the "A" in AMPERE.
