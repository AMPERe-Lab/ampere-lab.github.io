Prof. Gadiyar has been selected to receive the 2026 IEEE Andrew W. Smith Outstanding Young Member Achievement Award for his _contributions to advancing electric machines and electro-mechanical systems through multi-physics optimization, harmonic control, and modern manufacturing techniques._ 

This award, established 30 years ago in 1996, recognizes outstanding achievement and contribution to the profession by an IEEE Industry Applications Society (IEEE-IAS) member less than 35 years of age.
More details [here](https://ias.ieee.org/awards/ias-andrew-w-smith-outstanding-young-member-achievement-award/)
Established in the 1960s, IEEE-IAS is one of the top-10 largest technical societies within the IEEE, with over 20,000+ worldwide members and a network of over 530 chapters. It is one of the premier IEEE societies in the area of electric machines and their applications.
