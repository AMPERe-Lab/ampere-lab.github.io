Prof. Nishanth Gadiyar has received the Outstanding Associate Editor Award from the IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) for his work as an Associate Editor of the _IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications_ in 2025–2026.

Prof. Gadiyar has served as an Associate Editor since May 2025 and received the award in his first year of service.
