Prof. Gadiyar gave the lab's first conference talk at [ASME DRIVN 2026](https://drivn.secure-platform.com/a/solicitations/284/sessiongallery/24312/application/197621), ASME's Global Transportation and Power Systems Summit, in Detroit on September 14, 2026.

The talk, _Electric Machine Design Considerations for Heavy-Duty and Off-Highway Electrification_, was part of the session on electrification in agriculture, construction, and rail. It covered:

- what heavy-duty and off-highway vehicles demand from electric motors: high torque and power density, continuous high-load operation, harsh environments, and long service life
- the cost, supply-chain, and sustainability risks of relying on rare-earth permanent magnets
- reduced-rare-earth and rare-earth-free motor technologies, and how advances in electromagnetic design, thermal integration, controls, and manufacturing can close the performance gap
- examples of integrated electro-mechanical systems for off-highway vehicles, combining electrical, mechanical, thermal, and hydraulic design
